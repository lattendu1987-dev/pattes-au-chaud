Muddy paws: the gear that keeps your home clean after walks

Rain, wet leaves and soggy trails: in fall, every walk can end with paw prints all over the house. With a few well-chosen items and a quick routine at the door, you keep the house clean without stressing your dog.

Time-saving gear

  • Paw cleaner cup with silicone bristles: dip each paw with a little lukewarm water and the mud comes off in seconds.
  • Microfiber towel with hand pockets: far more absorbent than a regular towel.
  • Absorbent dog doormat: it traps water and dirt before they reach the rest of the house.
  • Dog drying robe: handy for large, long-haired dogs that come back soaked.

What to check

  • Cup size that matches your dog: a small one won't fit a big dog's paw.
  • Soft silicone with no sharp edges, easy to rinse.
  • Machine-washable towel and mat.
  • Non-slip backing on the mat to avoid slips on hard floors.

A quick routine at the door

  • Set the paw cleaner and towel by the door before you leave.
  • Reward your dog for cooperating: within days, many dogs lift their paw on their own.
  • Dry between the paw pads too, where moisture lingers longest.
