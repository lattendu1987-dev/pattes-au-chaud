Light-up collars and headlamps: see and be seen on night walks

Once the clocks go back, the evening walk often happens in the dark. A dark-coated dog becomes almost invisible to drivers and cyclists. A few simple items change everything.

Make your dog visible

  • LED light-up collar or harness: choose a USB-rechargeable model with steady and flashing modes.
  • Check the water resistance stated by the maker (rain, puddles) and the advertised battery life.
  • A clip-on light for the existing collar is a cheap way to start.
  • A vest or coat with reflective strips complements the light by bouncing back headlights.

See where you're walking

  • A headlamp keeps both hands free for the leash and bags.
  • Prefer a rechargeable model with several brightness levels and a red mode that doesn't dazzle others.
  • For you too, a reflective armband or jacket helps along roads.

Good habits

  • Recharge everything at a fixed time, for example after the evening walk.
  • Keep your dog on a leash near roads, even when visible.
  • Choose lit routes when possible.

FAQ

Does flashing bother the dog?

The light sits around the neck or on the harness, outside the dog's direct field of view. If your dog seems bothered, use the steady mode.
