Slow feeder bowl: how to slow down a dog or cat that eats too fast

Some dogs and cats finish their meal in seconds. A slow feeder turns mealtime into a small game and makes it last longer, which also keeps pets busy on days they go out less.

Main types

  • Maze bowl: ridges make your pet search for each bite of kibble.
  • Lick mat: ideal for wet food spread thinly.
  • Puzzle or multi-level feeder: for pets that quickly figured out the maze.

What to look for

  • A shape matched to the snout: flat-faced pets need shallow ridges.
  • A non-slip base or suction cups so it stays put.
  • Easy-to-clean material, ideally dishwasher-safe.
  • Gradual difficulty: start easy.

Good habits

  • Keep the same amount of food as before.
  • Stay nearby at first and put the bowl away if it gets damaged.
  • If your pet still eats very fast or seems uncomfortable, ask your vet.
