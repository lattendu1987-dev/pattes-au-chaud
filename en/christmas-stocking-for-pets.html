Christmas stocking for dogs and cats: what to put inside

A pet's Christmas stocking has become a little tradition in many homes. To make it a hit, pick gifts that suit your pet's size, age and habits.

Ideas for dogs

  • A sturdy chew toy matched to jaw strength.
  • A treat-dispensing toy or small snuffle mat.
  • A ball or rope toy to play together.
  • A few quality treats, in small amounts.

Ideas for cats

  • A feather wand toy to play together.
  • Light balls or fabric mice.
  • A small scratcher or catnip pillow.
  • A few cat-safe treats.

Safety rules

  • Toy size matched to your pet to avoid choking hazards.
  • No ribbon, string or wrapping paper left within reach.
  • Supervise the first uses of a new toy.
  • Treats count toward the day's food.

Can I give my pet holiday treats meant for people?

No: chocolate and many holiday foods aren't safe for pets. Pick treats made for them.
