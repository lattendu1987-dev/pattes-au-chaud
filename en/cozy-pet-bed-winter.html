Cozy pet bed: how to choose the right winter bed for your dog or cat

In winter, cold floors and drafts can make some beds uncomfortable. A good bed insulates from the floor, has sides to curl up against and is easy to wash.

What to look for

  • A thick, insulating base so the cold floor doesn't come through.
  • Raised sides or a donut shape for curling up.
  • A removable, machine-washable cover.
  • A non-slip or waterproof bottom.

Getting the size right

  • Measure your pet curled up and stretched out, then add a few inches.
  • Cats often like enclosed or igloo-style beds.
  • Large or senior dogs appreciate a firm, easy-access mattress.

Good habits

  • Keep the bed away from drafty doors and windows.
  • Don't push it against a radiator: your pet should be able to move away from the heat.
  • Wash the cover regularly.

Round or rectangular?

Pets that sleep curled up often prefer round beds with raised sides; those that stretch out like a rectangular mattress.
