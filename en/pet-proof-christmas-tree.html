Christmas tree and pets: the gear for a cat- and dog-proof tree

To a cat, a Christmas tree is a climbing frame covered in shiny toys. To a dog, it's in the path of a wagging tail. With a few well-chosen items, you can enjoy the holidays without breakage.

A stable tree

  • A heavy, wide stand, or a tree anchored to the wall or ceiling with a discreet tie.
  • A smaller tree on a table or sideboard can be easier with a keen climber.
  • A decorative gate or playpen around the base for dogs and puppies.

Pet-friendly decorations

  • Shatterproof ornaments (plastic or fabric) instead of glass.
  • Nothing edible or small on the lower branches.
  • Skip tinsel, which cats love to chew.
  • String lights with well-secured cords, unplugged when you're out.

Good habits

  • Put the tree up a few days before decorating so your pet gets used to it.
  • Cover the water reservoir of a real tree so pets can't drink from it.
  • Offer a toy or scratching post nearby as a distraction.
