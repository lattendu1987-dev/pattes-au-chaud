Pet hair everywhere: the tools that really work

Spring and fall shedding, the couch, dark clothes… There are simple tools to get back in control without buying sticky refills every week.

Start at the source

Regular brushing removes loose hair before it ends up on the furniture. It's the most effective step, especially during shedding season.

Tools by use

  • Reusable roller with a collection chamber: for couches and cushions, no refills needed.
  • Rubber brush or squeegee: works well on rugs and short-pile fabrics.
  • Grooming glove: for pets that dislike brushes.
  • Cat corner brush: fixes to a wall corner, the cat rubs against it and leaves its hair there.
  • Vacuum with a motorized pet brush: the most effective investment for large areas.
