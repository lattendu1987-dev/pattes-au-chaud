Dog sweater: how to choose one for small dogs that feel the cold

A sweater can help small dogs, short-haired breeds and seniors handle the cold, both at home and on dry winter walks. As long as it fits well and feels nice to wear.

Sweater or coat?

  • Sweater: soft and warm, ideal at home or in cold, dry weather.
  • Coat: waterproof, better for rain and snow.
  • Many owners keep both to match the weather.

What to look for

  • Soft material: fleece or thick knit, nothing scratchy.
  • The right fit: measure back length, chest and neck.
  • A turtleneck or high collar to protect the neck.
  • A harness opening if you walk your dog on a harness.
  • Machine washable.

Good habits

  • Get your dog used to it gradually, with treats.
  • Take it off indoors if it's warm or your dog is panting.
  • Check it doesn't restrict the legs or get in the way of potty breaks.
