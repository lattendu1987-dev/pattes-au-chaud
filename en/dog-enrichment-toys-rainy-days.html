Enrichment toys: keeping your dog busy on rainy days

When it's pouring or dark by late afternoon, walks get shorter. A bored dog can quickly start chewing whatever it finds. Games that use the nose and the brain often tire a dog as much as a walk.

Main types of games

  • Snuffle mats: hide kibble in the folds and let your dog sniff it out.
  • Treat-dispensing toys: a ball or cube that releases treats as the dog rolls it.
  • Puzzle toys: flaps to lift and pieces to slide, with several difficulty levels.
  • Stuffable, freezable toys: they keep dogs busy for a long time, great while you work from home.

What to look for

  • Size matched to your dog: big enough that it can't be swallowed.
  • Tough material for strong chewers, and easy to clean (dishwasher or washing machine).
  • Difficulty level: start easy so your dog succeeds quickly, then step it up.
  • Non-slip base under puzzles so they don't slide on hard floors.

Good habits

  • Use part of the daily food ration instead of adding extra treats.
  • Stay nearby the first few times, and put the toy away if it starts to break.
  • Rotate games from day to day to keep things interesting.
