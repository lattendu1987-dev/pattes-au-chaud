Cat tree: how to choose one for an indoor cat in winter

In winter, many cats go outside less and spend their days indoors. A good cat tree gives them somewhere to climb, scratch, watch and nap, and spares your couch at the same time.

What to look for

  • Stability: a wide, heavy base, or a wall anchor for tall models. A wobbly tree won't get used.
  • The right height: cats love a high lookout, but tall models must be secured.
  • Natural sisal scratching posts, tall enough for a full stretch.
  • Wide platforms and an enclosed hideaway for quiet naps.
  • Washable covering or removable cushions.

Matching your cat

  • Kittens and senior cats: low, closely spaced steps.
  • Large cats (like Maine Coons): wide platforms and thick posts.
  • Several cats: multiple beds and levels to avoid squabbles.

Good habits

  • Put it near a window: watching outside is many cats' favorite pastime.
  • Rub a little catnip on the posts to encourage use.
  • Check screws and anchors regularly.

FAQ

My cat ignores it. What should I do?

Move it (near a window or a favorite spot), add a little catnip and play with your cat around the tree.
