Holiday road trips with pets: the car travel essentials

During the holidays, lots of pets travel to visit family. In the car, a pet should be secured or in a carrier: it's safer for them and for everyone on board.

Gear by pet

  • Cat or small dog: a sturdy, well-ventilated carrier, wedged on the floor or secured with the seat belt.
  • Medium or large dog: a car safety harness clipped to the seat belt, or a crate in the trunk.
  • A waterproof seat or trunk cover to protect the car.

What to look for

  • The right size: your pet should be able to stand and turn around in the carrier.
  • A harness designed and tested for car travel, not just a walking harness.
  • A secure latch and comfortable handle on the carrier.

Good habits

  • Get your pet used to the carrier a few days ahead, with a blanket that smells like home.
  • Stop roughly every two hours for dogs, with water.
  • Never leave a pet alone in a car.

My cat hates the carrier. What can I do?

Leave it open at home for a few days beforehand, with a blanket and treats, so it becomes a familiar spot.
