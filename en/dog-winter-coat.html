Winter coat for dogs: a simple guide to choosing the right one

Not every dog needs a coat. But for small dogs, short-haired breeds, seniors and dogs that feel the cold, a good coat makes winter walks much more pleasant.

Does your dog need one?

Northern breeds with a double coat usually do fine without. Small, short-haired or older dogs tend to feel the cold more. If in doubt, ask your vet.

What to look for

  • Belly coverage: that's the part that touches the snow on small dogs.
  • A waterproof shell and a warm lining (fleece or quilted).
  • An opening for the harness if you walk your dog on a harness.
  • Reflective strips or details: it gets dark early in winter.
  • Machine washable.

How to measure

  • Back length: from the base of the neck to the base of the tail.
  • Chest: at the widest point, just behind the front legs.
  • Neck: where the collar sits.

FAQ

Should the coat be tight?

Snug but not restrictive: you should be able to slide two fingers under the straps.
