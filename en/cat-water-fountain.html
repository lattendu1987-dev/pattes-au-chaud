Cat water fountain: how to choose one and keep it clean easily

Many cats prefer drinking running water. A fountain can encourage them to drink more often, especially in winter when heating dries the air. It just needs to be quiet and easy to clean.

What to look for

  • Quiet operation: a noisy pump can scare your cat.
  • A capacity that matches the number of pets.
  • Easy-to-wash material: stainless steel, ceramic or BPA-free plastic.
  • Filters that are easy to find and replace.
  • Automatic shut-off when the water runs low.

Cleaning

  • Change the water regularly, even though it filters.
  • Wash the fountain and pump as the maker recommends.
  • Replace the filter on schedule.

Good habits

  • Keep it away from the food bowl and litter box.
  • Leave the old water bowl out for a few days while your cat adjusts.
  • Route the cord out of reach of claws and teeth.
