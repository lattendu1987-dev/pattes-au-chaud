Advent calendar for dogs or cats: how to pick the right one

Pet advent calendars have become a December classic. They're not all equal: here's what to check before ordering, and when to order.

Order early

Advent calendars are seasonal: the most popular ones often sell out in November. To open it on December 1st, order by mid-November.

Treats or toys?

  • Treats: check the ingredient list, especially if your pet has allergies or a special diet.
  • Toys: make sure they suit your pet's size to avoid choking hazards.
  • Mixed: a good compromise, often slightly more expensive.

What to look for

  • A calendar made for the right species: dog treats don't suit cats, and vice versa.
  • Reasonable portions: treats add to the day's food ration.
  • A best-before date that covers the whole of December.

FAQ

Can my pet have a treat every day?

Yes, if portions stay small and you deduct them from the daily ration. If in doubt, ask your vet.
